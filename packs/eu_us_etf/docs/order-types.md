An order is an instruction submitted to a trading venue or intermediary. Different order types express different constraints, and the available choices depend on the service and market. A market order generally prioritizes execution over a specified price limit, while a limit order includes a maximum acceptable purchase price or a minimum acceptable sale price. An order may execute only in part, may not execute, or may execute under conditions the person did not expect. Market structures and order handling can differ. This document is conceptual and does not provide instructions for placing an actual order.

There is no order-routing function in this repository. DemoBroker is a fictional example, and the tools do not connect to brokers or exchanges. If a reader has a question about a real service, they should consult that service's official documentation rather than assume this demo's wording applies.
