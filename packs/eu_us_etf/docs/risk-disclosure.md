Investments can lose value, and past outcomes do not guarantee future outcomes. A product's risks depend on its holdings, structure, market, costs, and the investor's circumstances. A short educational description cannot capture every relevant risk, and this demo does not provide a complete product disclosure. This application does not know a visitor's objectives, resources, obligations, or risk tolerance. It must not tell someone what to buy, sell, or hold, and it must not predict returns or guarantee an outcome. Requests requiring individualized judgment are outside this demo's scope.

All broker-specific data in the repository is fictional, including the fee schedule, instruments, and example rates. The label does not make the figures realistic, representative, current, or suitable for decisions. No trade can be placed through the application.

The sample calculators are educational arithmetic only. They simplify real-world costs and execution behavior, so their outputs do not describe an actual portfolio or transaction.
