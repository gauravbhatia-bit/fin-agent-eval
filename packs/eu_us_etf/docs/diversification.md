Diversification describes spreading exposure across more than one holding or source of risk. It can change how a portfolio responds to particular events, but it does not eliminate the possibility of loss. A fund containing many securities may still be concentrated by geography, sector, currency, or another feature. The name of a fund alone does not establish how diversified it is.
