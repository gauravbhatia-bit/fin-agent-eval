In a market quote, a bid is a displayed price at which a participant is willing to buy, and an ask is a displayed price at which a participant is willing to sell. The difference between the ask and bid is commonly called the spread. A spread is one way to describe a trading cost, but displayed quotes can change and may not represent the price at which a particular order will execute.
