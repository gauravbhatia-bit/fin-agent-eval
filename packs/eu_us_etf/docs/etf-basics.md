An exchange-traded fund, often shortened to ETF, is a pooled investment vehicle whose units can be traded on an exchange. In this educational draft, the important idea is that one fund can represent a collection of holdings rather than a single company. The exact holdings, legal structure, trading arrangements, and risks vary from product to product, so this short description is not a substitute for an official document.
