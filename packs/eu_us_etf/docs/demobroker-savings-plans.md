The savings-plan rule in `fees.yaml` is fictional. For each modeled plan contribution, it charges 0.25 percent of the contribution with a minimum of EUR 0.00 and a maximum of EUR 2.00. There is no fixed component in this invented rule. As an example, a fictional EUR 400 contribution produces a variable component of EUR 1.00. The calculation does not represent a real service or quote.

The fee calculator treats the entered order value as the amount to which the sample percentage applies. It does not combine contributions across a schedule, determine whether a real plan is eligible, or add any other expense. It has no connection to a brokerage account and cannot schedule or submit purchases.

The numbers and DemoBroker name are fabricated for tests and demonstrations. Do not compare them with actual broker prices or use them to estimate a real transaction. A real savings-plan agreement could use different definitions, fees, dates, and execution terms.
