DemoBroker is a fictional name used only by this repository. It has no support team, telephone number, email address, customer portal, account system, or business hours. Any question asking for DemoBroker support details should be answered by explaining that the organization and its service information are made up for a software demo.

The application cannot look up a real person's account, verify identity, open a real support case, or contact a human. Its later escalation tool returns a deterministic local placeholder ticket identifier and explicitly does not contact anyone. Never describe that placeholder as a real support ticket.

This document is intentionally short and distinct from the fee and instrument records. It allows tests to verify that knowledge retrieval can identify the fictional support topic without inventing phone numbers or pretending that an actual company exists. All content is fictional demo data and not a representation of any real broker.
