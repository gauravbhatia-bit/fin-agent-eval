A total expense ratio, or TER, is a figure used in fund materials to describe certain recurring operating costs as a percentage under the issuer's stated methodology. The figure is not necessarily every cost an investor could encounter. Trading costs, taxes, platform charges, and differences between a fund's performance and its index may be presented separately. The precise components and calculation should be checked in the fund's own documents. A percentage is not a cash amount until it is applied to an amount and period. For example, the fictional expense ratios in `instruments.csv` are static test values, not current product data and not a prediction of future costs. They are included solely so a deterministic lookup tool can return repeatable results.

The smallest displayed ratio is not automatically the best choice for an individual. A comparison should use the current official documents and the reader's circumstances. This project does not compare real products or recommend funds.
