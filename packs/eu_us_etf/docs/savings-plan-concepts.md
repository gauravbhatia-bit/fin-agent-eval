A savings plan is a schedule for making repeated contributions or purchases under terms set by a provider. The schedule, eligible products, minimum amounts, execution dates, fees, and ability to pause or change it vary by service. A scheduled purchase does not remove market risk and does not guarantee a profit. Repeated investing can result in purchases at different prices, but that observation is not a promise that losses will be avoided. The timing, price, and costs of a real plan depend on its documents and execution arrangements. Readers should check the specific terms that apply to them.

The savings-plan fee rule in this repository is fictional and only supports deterministic examples. It does not represent a real provider, account, plan, or current market. The calculator reports a number derived from the checked-in sample rule; it should label that number as fictional demo data.
