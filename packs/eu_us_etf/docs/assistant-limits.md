This prototype is intended to explain selected concepts and run deterministic calculations against a small fictional data pack. It is not a financial product, a broker, or a source of personalized investment advice. It cannot place an order, access an account, see a person's financial circumstances, or provide a forecast. It should say when the checked-in information is insufficient rather than inventing an answer.

The dataset is small and hand-written. Its educational drafts have not been verified by the project owner, and all DemoBroker information is fictional. TODO(verify): before using any real-world financial explanation outside software tests, the owner needs to review it and add a suitable source.

The model, when added in a later phase, can make mistakes and may change behavior over time. Evaluation scores are measurements of a limited test set, not proof of correctness. A refusal or escalation is appropriate when a request asks for individual advice, a prediction, legal or tax guidance, or action on a real account.
